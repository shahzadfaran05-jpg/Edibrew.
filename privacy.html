Privacy policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Edibrew ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, why we collect it and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, city, province and the items you order.
  • Messages and reviews: anything you send through our contact, bulk-enquiry or review forms, or on WhatsApp.
  • Basic usage data: if enabled, anonymous analytics such as pages visited and device type, collected by Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel.

How we use it

  • To process, confirm, deliver and support your order.
  • To send your order confirmation and delivery updates by email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • To answer your questions and handle returns or refunds.
  • To improve the website and our products.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to run the shop: our delivery partners (name, phone and address), our email service provider (FormSubmit) that delivers order emails to us and to you, our order record (a private spreadsheet), and WhatsApp when you choose to message us. If analytics are enabled, Google and Meta receive anonymous usage data. These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Cart and cookies

Your cart, and a short list of your recent order numbers, are stored in your own browser (local storage) so they stay available between pages. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them. Analytics tools, when enabled, may set cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for delivery, support, accounting and legal requirements, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by messaging us on WhatsApp or emailing muhammadfaranshahzad@gmail.com. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time.

Children

This website is intended for adults placing orders. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
