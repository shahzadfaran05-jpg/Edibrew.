BUYER CONFIRMATION EMAIL - SETUP (free, about 10 minutes)
=========================================================
Every order already sends a basic confirmation to the buyer through FormSubmit.
For a proper branded email with the right subject line, use EmailJS (free: 200 emails a month).

1. Go to emailjs.com and create a free account.
2. Email Services > Add New Service > choose Gmail > connect muhammadfaranshahzad@gmail.com. Copy the SERVICE ID (looks like service_xxxxxxx).
3. Email Templates > Create New Template. Fill in:
     To Email:   {{to_email}}
     From Name:  Edibrew
     Reply To:   {{reply_to}}
     Subject:    Your Edibrew order {{order_id}} is received
     Content:    click the "<>" (code) button and paste the whole content of emailjs-template.html
   Save, then copy the TEMPLATE ID (looks like template_xxxxxxx).
4. Account > General > copy your PUBLIC KEY.
5. Open config.js on GitHub (pencil icon) and fill in:
     EMAILJS: { SERVICE_ID: 'service_xxxxxxx', TEMPLATE_ID: 'template_xxxxxxx', PUBLIC_KEY: 'your_public_key' },
   Commit changes.
6. Place a test order with your own email. You should receive the confirmation within a minute. Check spam once.

If EMAILJS is left empty, the site uses the basic FormSubmit confirmation instead.
Order emails to YOU always come through FormSubmit (activate it once, see README.txt).
