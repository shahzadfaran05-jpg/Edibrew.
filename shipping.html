Shipping & payment

Delivery across Pakistan.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Delivery charges

A flat Rs 150 delivery charge is added to every order. It is shown in your cart and in your order confirmation before you pay.

Delivery times

WhereEstimated time
Lahore2 to 3 days
Rest of Punjab5 days
Rest of Pakistan7 days
Vendors and bulk orders7 days

Times count from order confirmation, are estimates, and can be longer during holidays or bad weather. We deliver inside Pakistan only.

Payment options

  • Cash on delivery: pay the courier when your parcel arrives.
  • Mobile wallet or bank transfer: shown at checkout when available. Send the payment, then share the screenshot with your order number on WhatsApp.

Track your order

You will receive an order number (for example EB-1234567) as soon as you order. Use it on the Track your order page or message us on WhatsApp for a status update.

Packaging

Edible cups are fragile. We pack them to protect their shape in transit. Please keep the parcel upright and open it carefully.

See our Returns & refunds policy or contact us.
